01 / About

Quality beyond testing.

I approach QA as more than finding defects. My role is to understand how a product is expected to work, identify where it is most likely to fail, design effective coverage, investigate unexpected behaviour, and give teams the confidence to release.

My experience spans data-driven platforms, education technology, e-commerce, enterprise systems, humanitarian technology, HRMS, accounting, mobile applications, and API-led products.

I work closely with engineers, product managers, business analysts, designers, and other stakeholders to build quality into the delivery process instead of treating QA as the last gate before release.

04 / Approach

How I approach quality.

A repeatable process for turning uncertainty into release confidence.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Product goals, users, requirements, architecture, and expected business outcomes.

  2. 02

    Identify Risk

    Critical workflows, complex rules, integrations, edge cases, and high-impact failure points.

  3. 03

    Design

    The right blend of UI, API, data, integration, exploratory, regression, and automation coverage.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Behaviour across layers, rather than trusting only what appears on the screen.

  5. 05

    Investigate

    Use DevTools, APIs, logs, environment comparison, and SQL to narrow down failures.

  6. 06

    Release

    Communicate what was tested, what remains risky, and whether the product is ready.
